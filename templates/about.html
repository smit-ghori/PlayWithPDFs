About PlayWithPDFs

Built to simplify your PDF workflow

PlayWithPDFs is a modern PDF toolkit created to make working with PDF documents simple, fast, and accessible for everyone.

The Story

PlayWithPDFs was independently designed and developed by a solo developer with a passion for building clean, modern, and useful web applications.

Every feature — from PDF merging and compression to conversion and security tools — has been carefully created to provide a smooth and reliable experience.

What You Can Do

  • Merge and organize PDF files
  • Compress PDF documents
  • Convert files to and from PDF
  • Protect and unlock PDFs
  • OCR and intelligent PDF tools
  • Edit, sign, crop, and watermark PDFs

Focused on Simplicity

The goal of PlayWithPDFs is to offer powerful PDF tools with a modern interface, fast performance, and an easy workflow without unnecessary complexity.

Everything is designed to feel clean, responsive, and professional across desktop and mobile devices.

Built with Passion

PlayWithPDFs is not backed by a large company or team. It is a continuously evolving project built with dedication, learning, and attention to detail.

Every improvement is focused on creating a better experience for users worldwide.

DEVELOPER

PlayWithPDFs was fully designed and developed as a solo project with a focus on modern UI, performance, and user experience.
